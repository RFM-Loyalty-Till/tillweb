An estimate, not a quote. Card fees are monthly card sales × rate × 12, with the credit and debit rates weighed by the debit share. All prices are before VAT; 5% VAT applies to provider fees, including the MDR, on both sides. Outside supplier prices are published benchmarks and can be changed above; actual quotes will vary.

Where these benchmarks come from
  • Card rates: Mashreq NEOPAY (NEO BIZ) lists 1.97% for UAE cards and 2.70% for international cards; Mashreq's tariff goes up to 3.00%. kredit.ae
  • Debit cards: since October 2024 the UAE Central Bank caps the issuer's share (interchange) on local debit cards at 0.75%, so debit-heavy restaurants may be quoted lower rates. Adyen
  • Device rent: e& UTap from AED 64.05 a month; Mashreq up to AED 1,000 to 2,000 a year per device. e&
  • Handheld app: Syrve Waiter, AED 50 per device per month. Syrve
  • Restaurant system, full-featured tiers: Foodics Advanced AED 556 a month, Syrve up to AED 450 a month per till, Geidea up to AED 364 a month. Geidea, Foodics
  • Loyalty: Square Loyalty top tier USD 105 (AED 385) per location a month. Square
  • Screens: no outside supplier offers in-venue screens like OOHUP. Do-it-yourself screen software such as Yodeck starts at USD 8 (AED 29) per screen a month, screen not included. Yodeck
  • Also ask the other provider about settlement time (T+1 means the money arrives the next working day) and chargeback fees. Prices ex VAT, USD at AED 3.6725, from published pages and listings, November 2026.
